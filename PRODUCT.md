# Aditya Mer — Portfolio

## Register
brand

## Product Purpose
Awwwards-nominated portfolio for an ML/AI engineer that proves technical sophistication through its own design and interactivity. Serves three audiences: recruiters hiring ML/AI engineers, clients seeking GenAI development, and the academic/research community.

## Users
- **Recruiters** — evaluating ML/AI engineering talent
- **Clients** — seeking GenAI development services
- **Researchers** — reviewing publications and technical depth

## Brand Personality
Editorial, precise, confident, technically sophisticated. The tone of a high-end design publication merged with developer tooling precision. Not playful, not corporate, not academic — a third thing.

## Anti-References
- ❌ No hero image of the person
- ❌ No loud gradient backgrounds (a subtle monochrome 3D lattice is the only ambient layer)
- ❌ No "funky" or brutalist design
- ❌ No bouncing / elastic UI animations
- ❌ No gradient text
- ❌ No glassmorphism
- ❌ No modal as default interaction
- ❌ No rainbow color — strictly ink/paper with restrained rust accent

## Signature Elements (allowed, on-brand)
- ✅ Monochrome point-cloud 3D background that reacts to scroll (subtle, reduced-motion aware)
- ✅ Pinned horizontal scroll for selected projects (desktop)
- ✅ Custom ink cursor with lagging ring
- ✅ Editorial typography (Fraunces display, Inter body, JetBrains Mono labels)

## Strategic Principles
1. The site itself is proof of what Aditya builds
2. Every animation serves a narrative purpose
3. White space is generous and intentional
4. Scroll experience is the first case study
5. /lab page is the secret weapon — live AI demos
