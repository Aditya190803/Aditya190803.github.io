06FAQ

Common questions.

How do you price a project?

A fixed quote per project, given after our call. You get a written proposal before any work starts.

Can my team update the site themselves?

Yes. You get an editor for news, events, products and team pages, and a walkthrough is included.

Do you work with clients outside Mumbai?

Yes. We work over calls and preview links, with hours that overlap yours.

Who owns the code and data?

You do, once the work is delivered and paid for.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, happy to.

What about support after launch?

Either optional monthly hosting, updates and backups, or a full handover to your team. Your choice.
