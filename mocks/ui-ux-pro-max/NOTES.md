# ui-ux-pro-max mock: notes

**Design summary:** A warm-paper Swiss/minimal portfolio in deep teal and logo coral. Space Grotesk headings, Archivo body, a numbered index column for each section, and thin concentric "orbit" rings with a single coral sun as the background graphic.

- **Design-system run** (`search.py "freelance developer agency portfolio light minimal" --design-system --variance 5 --motion 3 --density 3`): it suggested Brutalism with a pink palette. That is too loud for a business-facing freelancer and ignores the "simple" brief, so I only kept its dial outputs: a spacious density scale and subtle 300–400 ms scroll reveals with a small 12px y-offset.
- **Product domain** (`"freelancer developer portfolio"`): it recommends **Flat Design + Minimalism & Swiss Style** and a **Feature-Rich Showcase + Conversion** landing. I used that as the main direction: a 12-column grid, sharp hierarchy, one primary accent, no gradients, and hairline rules instead of cards.
- **Style row (minimalism-and-swiss-style)**: subtle 200–250 ms hovers, no unnecessary decoration, and max-width about 1200px. I applied these as a sticky left "index" column (01 Client work … 07 Contact) with ruled lists, all very Swiss.
- **Palette** (color domain, "Trust teal" row): primary `#0F766E` teal, chosen on purpose to move away from the rejected blue. I paired it with the coral from Aditya's own logo for graphics (`#E8673F`) and a darker coral for text (`#B9431C`, at least 4.5:1). Everything sits on warm paper `#F5F2EA`. All colours are semantic CSS tokens.
- **Typography** (typography domain, "Minimalist Portfolio" pairing): Space Grotesk for headings and Archivo for body text, which replaces the rejected Schibsted Grotesk. Body text is 17px with 1.6 line-height, and headings use `text-wrap: balance`.
- **Landing patterns**: from "Portfolio Grid" I took visuals first, a neutral background and a minimal accent. From "Funnel" I took repeated mini-CTAs: hero, Theska "ask on a call", Flier, after Process, then a final contact band in dark teal.
- **Background graphics**: instead of the rejected square grid, thin concentric rings with one filled disc appear in the hero (with a slow-orbiting coral dot), Flier and Contact. The Theska private card reuses the rings, and the Process step dots echo them.
- **UX checklist (quick-reference §1–§3)**: skip link; visible 3px focus rings; `scroll-padding-top` so the sticky header doesn't hide focus; 44–48px touch targets on nav, buttons, FAQ rows and footer links; SVG icons only (no emoji), with decorative ones `aria-hidden`; labelled form fields with hint text and inline validation via `aria-invalid` and a polite live region; `details/summary` FAQ; and `alt` text, width/height and lazy-loading on screenshots (no layout shift).
- **Motion and responsiveness**: the reveal content stays visible with no JS. `prefers-reduced-motion` turns off the orbit animation, smooth scroll and reveals. Layout collapses at 960px and 860px, with a hamburger menu that has `aria-expanded` and closes on Esc. Checked at 1440 and 390 with no horizontal scroll.
- **Honesty rules**: no testimonials, stats or logos beyond the brief. The Tata Memorial line is a dashed, labelled `data-removable` block ("Removable line · unverified").
