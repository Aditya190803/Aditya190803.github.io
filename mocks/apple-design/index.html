FAQ

Good questions.

How do you price?

A fixed quote per project, after a short call. You get a written proposal before any work starts.

Can my team update the site?

Yes. You get an editor for news, events, products and team pages, and a walkthrough is included.

Do you work with clients outside Mumbai?

Yes — over calls and preview links, with working hours that overlap with yours.

Who owns the code and data?

You do, once the project is delivered and paid for.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes, happy to.

What about support after launch?

Your choice: optional monthly hosting, updates and backups, or a full handover to your team.
