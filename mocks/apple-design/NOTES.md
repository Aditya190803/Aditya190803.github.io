**Design summary:** warm paper-white page with ink type and a single persimmon accent (taken from the AM monogram), soft light blooms and thin contour rings behind the content, translucent glass chrome, and a client-work carousel driven by springs that you can drag, flick and grab mid-motion.

- **Typography (§15):** system font first (SF Pro on Apple devices, Inter with optical sizing elsewhere). Tracking depends on size: -0.035em on the display headline, -0.028em on section titles, about 0 on body text, and slightly positive on small labels. Line height gets tighter as size grows (1.02 at display size, 1.55 for body). Hierarchy comes from weight, size and leading together. Spacing is in `rem`, so the layout scales with the user's text size.
- **Response (§1):** buttons scale to 0.965 on pointer-down (`:active` plus a pointerdown class for touch) with an 80ms transition. `touch-action: manipulation` removes the tap delay.
- **Direct manipulation and interruptibility (§2–3):** the carousel follows the pointer 1:1 from where it was grabbed, using pointer capture and a 10px hysteresis that lets vertical scrolling win. Grabbing a card mid-animation takes the spring's live value, so it never jumps.
- **Springs, velocity handoff and momentum (§4–6):** a custom spring uses Apple's damping and response parameters. It is critically damped (1.0) for button and dot navigation and 0.82 only after a real flick. The release velocity is passed to the spring, and the resting point is projected with Apple's `(v/1000)·d/(1−d)` formula (d = 0.998) before snapping.
- **Rubber-banding (§9):** dragging past the first or last card meets increasing resistance (`rubberband(overshoot, width, 0.55)`) instead of a hard stop.
- **Materials (§12):** the nav is a floating translucent pill (blur 22px, saturate 180%, bright inset top edge). There is no 1px divider; a soft blur and gradient mask fades in where content scrolls under the chrome. Bigger surfaces (the Flier and contact cards) get deeper shadows than small chips. The form is a single glass layer over a gradient, never glass on glass.
- **Spatial consistency and motion (§7, §11):** FAQ answers open and close along the same path with the Web Animations API, starting from the current height so they can be interrupted. Content reveals by materialising (opacity, blur and a 14px rise together) rather than just fading. Only transform, opacity and filter are animated.
- **Accessibility (§14):** under `prefers-reduced-motion`, springs snap instantly, reveals become opacity-only fades and press scaling is removed. `prefers-reduced-transparency` makes glass surfaces solid, and `prefers-contrast: more` darkens hairlines and secondary text. The background graphics are static, with no looping oscillation.
- **Foundations (§16):** specific nav labels (Work, Flier, Services…), status feedback (an availability chip and an aria-live form status), inline validation on blur rather than on submit, a keyboard-operable carousel (arrows, dots, ←/→), and a skip link. The unverified Tata Memorial line is marked `data-removable`.
