**Design summary:** "Mail merge" portfolio: a cool sage-white page with pine-green ink, where the hero sentence is a live template whose marigold `{{merge field}}` takes its value from a small spreadsheet you click. Faint postmark rings and wavy cancellation lines provide the background graphics.

- **Grounded in the subject.** The visual language comes from Aditya's own product (Flier, a mail-merge app) and from post. The page uses `{{variables}}`, CSV rows, postmark rings and cancellation waves, with "Mumbai, India / sent anywhere" set around the stamp.
- **Boldness spent in one place.** The signature is the hero's merge field. On load it merges rows 1 to 4 once (one orchestrated moment), then the visitor can click any row to swap it. Everything else (rules, plain lists, quiet pills) stays restrained.
- **Avoided the calibration defaults.** The background is a cool sage-white (#F5F7F4), not cream. Accents are pine #1E6A50 and marigold #F4B63F, with no terracotta, no acid-on-black and no blue. There are no all-caps eyebrows, no middle-dot meta strings, no arrows on buttons, and no identical shadowed SaaS cards.
- **Clearly different from the rejected design.** That design had a blue #2456d6 accent, Schibsted Grotesk and a square-grid background. This one uses Young Serif display, Instrument Sans body and organic wave or ring graphics, with no grid.
- **Typography as an active element.** Young Serif carries headlines at a large, tight-leading scale. JetBrains Mono appears only where the content really is template syntax (`{{first_name}}`), so the monospace face means something. Body line length is kept under about 60ch.
- **Structure as information.** Numbered markers appear only in Process, which is a real sequence. Client work uses heavy top rules, and credentials use a definition list (Research, Open source, Education, Experience) instead of decorative labels.
- **Real content only.** All copy comes from CONTENT.md, with no testimonials, stats or logos. The Tata Memorial line is wrapped in an HTML `REMOVABLE` comment, and I removed an invented reply-time promise during the self-review.
- **Plain, user-facing copy.** It is written in sentence case with active CTAs. "Start a project" keeps the same name everywhere. "Send message" produces "Message sent", and form errors say exactly what to fix.
- **Quality floor.** The page works from 390px to 1440px with no horizontal scroll. It has a skip link, visible focus rings, `aria-pressed` on the merge rows, a live region for the merged value, a native `details` FAQ and labelled form fields. With prefers-reduced-motion, the auto-merge, line drawing and stamp rotation are all turned off.
- **Self-critique passes.** Pass 1 left the hero's right side empty, so I moved the spreadsheet into a right column under the postmark. In the Flier section, the screenshot's shadow disappeared against the panel, so the shadow is now white and the template letter overlaps the screenshot. I also added balanced wrapping to headings to fix a one-word widow.
