# Taste-skill mock: notes

**Design summary:** a warm bone-paper page with chocolate type in Newsreader (serif display) and Hanken Grotesk, moss green kept for the main action and one flat block, and three quiet background graphics in CSS: a green and peach top glow, a dusk wash and rising arcs.

- **The kind decides everything (SKILL §1):** this is classed as *service* (people are hired), so the page follows the service plan: hero, work, product split, services rows, steps, credentials, FAQ, booking form and `footer.closing`. Because the trade is technical and the owner asked for background graphics, the page uses the skill's *soft CSS grounds* (`topglow` on the hero, `dusk` on About, `arcs` behind the close). These pair with each other in `backgrounds/index.json`. All of them are still, and the page has no shader, WebGL, grain or loop.
- **Deal, not taste (SKILL §4):** the hero (`word`), nav, close, footer, rhythm (`space`), button look (`arrow`) and mark (`wordmark`) were dealt at random from the service lists. Any part that needed material the brief lacks was dealt again, and the reasons are in the plan comment at the top of `<body>`. The palette `green-mat-chocolate` and the pairing `newsreader` (rated 3 for service) were taken from `library/`. They differ on purpose from the rejected blue `#2456d6` and Schibsted look.
- **One section per screen (layout-v4 §2):** at 1440x900 every section is exactly 900px tall with its h2 120px down, and only the hero shows on the first screen. On phones and short screens the sections size to their content instead. Forced second screens left half-empty tiles there.
- **Equal edges and one split line:** one 1280 `.wrap` (x 80 to 1360), a 12-column grid, and every split puts its text on the left line and its visual at x 732, never mirrored.
- **Plain copy (copy.md):** the h1 names the category and the place ("Web and AI developer, Mumbai"), and the first screen carries hard facts (Mumbai, IASCC 2025, IEEE TENSYMP 2024). Headings are nouns, buttons are a verb and an object, and the main action "Start a project" reads the same in the nav, hero, form and close. Nothing is invented: there are no testimonials, stats or logos, and the Tata Memorial line is marked `REMOVABLE` in a comment.
- **No captions under pictures, and proof as small exact objects:** project names sit inside the card padding above each screenshot. Credentials appear as linked citation cards (IEEE, CRC Press, PyPI) rather than a stat strip.
- **One system:** one card recipe (bone `#f3f0ea`, a 1px ink line at 13%, 20px radius), one filled button, one hairline, one link look. Colour stays more than 90% neutral, with green spent only on the action, the links and the Flier frame.
- **Hover layer only (motion.md):** a linked card lifts 2px, screenshots zoom 1.03 over 600ms, buttons step up 1px and press at .985, the arrow nudges 3px, underlines draw, and focus rings settle from 6px to 2px. Nothing moves on scroll or by time, and under `prefers-reduced-motion` only colours change.
- **Accessibility:** a skip link, landmarks, a labelled full-screen menu (Escape closes it), real `<label>`s with 48px fields, alt text on every screenshot, and ink at 12.5:1 with muted text at 4.5:1 or more.
- **Not followed:** the skill's own scripts (`consistency.mjs`, `check.mjs`, `icons.mjs`) were not run, as instructed. Checks used Playwright screenshots and section-geometry measurements instead. The owner's raster `logo.png` (an orange "AM" on cream) was left out in favour of the wordmark, and an SVG version would slot into `.wordmark`.
