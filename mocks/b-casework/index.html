.faq

What clients ask before the first call06
How do you price a project?

Each project gets a fixed quote based on its scope. After our call I send a written proposal with deliverables, timeline and price, so you know the full cost first.

Can my team update the site after launch?

Yes. Content you change often, such as news, events, products or team members, gets an editor, and I walk your team through it.

Do you work with clients outside Mumbai?

Yes. The work happens over calls and shared preview links, so I work with clients anywhere and overlap with your hours for calls.

Who owns the code and the data?

You do. Your data stays yours throughout, and once the project is delivered and paid for, the code and documentation are yours too.

Will you sign an NDA?

Yes. I’m happy to sign one before you share details about your product, data or business.

Do you look after the site once it’s live?

If you’d like me to. I can handle hosting, updates, backups and small changes each month, or hand everything over to your team.
